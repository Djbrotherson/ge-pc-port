# Code of Conduct

## Standard

Contributors are expected to keep project interactions professional,
technical, and respectful.

Disagreement about implementation is normal. Critique code, evidence,
assumptions, and trade-offs rather than people.

## Expected behavior

- provide reproducible technical evidence where possible,
- keep reviews focused on the change under discussion,
- respect licensing and content-distribution boundaries,
- do not publish private information, credentials, ROMs, or proprietary game
  assets,
- do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or deliberately disrupt other
  contributors.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, close, or remove contributions that violate
these expectations and may restrict participation for repeated or serious
abuse.

Security-sensitive reports should use the private process described in
`SECURITY.md`.
